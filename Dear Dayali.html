Open when…

Open whenstudying feels endless

Put the book down for five minutes and breathe. You are not behind. You are building something that takes years, and nobody builds that without getting tired. Drink some water, then come back to it.

Open whenyou doubt yourself

Think of the girl with the flower crown who looked into the mirror and was sure. She was right. Doubt is just what a big dream feels like from the inside. I have never once doubted you.

Open whenyou need a smile

Step outside, find something green, and take one slow breath. Call a friend, play a song you love, eat something sweet. Good days are built from small things like these.

Open whenthe white coat day arrives

Take a moment to look back at every late night and every exam that led there. The day will be well earned, and it will be a proud one.
